CivicBrands 8-Week Social Body Copy
Schedule window: 8 JUN 2026 - 31 JUL 2026
Cadence: Weekdays, Monday through Friday
Use: Facebook and Instagram body copy. Graphics handled separately.

---

MON 8 JUN 2026

CivicBrands is here to make useful public information easier to find, check, reuse, and correct.

We build civic infrastructure for the people who actually use it: directories, public records, resource pages, and practical tools that help responsibility travel upward.

Start here: civicbrands.org

---

TUE 9 JUN 2026

Kansas City is one metro across two states. That matters when people are looking for help.

The CivicBrands KC directory covers food, housing, healthcare, legal aid, crisis lines, transit, utilities, local government, and more across the bi-state metro.

civicbrands.org/kc

---

WED 10 JUN 2026

Public information should not come with a hidden invoice.

CivicBrands does not run ads, does not use third-party trackers, and does not sell data. The point is to make useful information easier to reach, not to turn need into a lead-generation funnel.

civicbrands.org/privacy

THU 11 JUN 2026

If a number stops working, tell us.

Wrong hours, disconnected phones, missing services, and "they turned me away" reports all matter. Corrections help the next person show up with better information.

Send updates: civicbrands.org/kc/suggest

FRI 12 JUN 2026

MON 15 JUN 2026

Entries in the KC directory carry confidence labels like Verified, Community-confirmed, Legacy, Unverified, and Seasonal. Not all public information has the same level of certainty, and pretending otherwise does not help anyone.
