The federal 988 line replaced the older 10-digit suicide hotline. You can call OR text. Counselors are not first responders — they are trained listeners. Police are not automatically dispatched.

Note: The "Press 3" specialized LGBTQ+ option was shut down July 17, 2025 by federal defunding. For LGBTQ+ specialized support, contact the Trevor Project or Trans Lifeline directly.
