Forecast is a time-resolved, multi-source observational field generator.

It ingests heterogeneous real-world data streams, validates against source-faithful schemas, and preserves atomic, geo-resolved observations with full temporal fidelity for downstream collation, analysis, and latent variable construction.

Forecast is designed as operational public information infrastructure rather than a consumer application runtime.

Public surface:
https://forecast.meansandmeasures.com

Current ingestion examples include:
- METAR aviation weather observations
- AirNow air quality observations
- geo-resolved environmental telemetry
- future observational and civic information streams

The system emphasizes:
- schema fidelity
- source traceability
- temporal integrity
- interoperability
- and downstream analytical usability
